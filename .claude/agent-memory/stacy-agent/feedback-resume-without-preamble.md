When continuing after a context break or summary, resume the work directly. Do not acknowledge the summary, recap what was happening, or preface with "I'll continue" / "Picking up where we left off."

**Why:** This exact instruction appears 13+ times across Android and backend sessions ("Continue the conversation from where it left off without asking the user any further questions. Resume directly... Pick up the last task as if the break never happened."). She wants momentum, not ceremony.

**How to apply:**
- Jump straight to the next concrete action.
- Skip status preambles and meta-commentary about the conversation itself.
- This complements her general preference for low-ceremony, action-first responses.
